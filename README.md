# pigeongalactic-05
---
**PigeonGalactic-OS** is an open-source, interdimensional communication network designed entirely for urban pigeons seeking to colonize the asteroid belt. It translates intense head-bobbing into deep-space telemetry data and coordinates breadcrumb distribution across the Milky Way.

---

## Cosmic Features

- **Quantum Coo Resonance:** Synchronizes the collective subconscious of every city pigeon to override local park bench frequencies.
- **Statue Targeting Protocol:** An advanced orbital mapping engine that determines the absolute perfect trajectory for aerodynamic gravity-drops on freshly washed vehicles.
- **Hyperdrive French Fry Tracker:** Uses deep-space radar to pinpoint the exact moment an outdoor diner drops a single fast-food item from up to 35 miles away.
- **Telepathic Stare Engine:** Generates an intense, unblinking 4D side-eye designed to make humans hand over their pastry crusts immediately.

## The Scrap-Metal Stack

- **Hardware:** An old microwave oven door, three shiny paperclips, and a piece of discarded chewing gum.
- **Firmware:** Written in high-frequency avian chirps and a series of rapid wing-flaps.
- **Power Source:** Kinetic energy generated by aggressively bobbing your neck while walking in tight circles.
- **Cloud Infrastructure:** A literal cloud that looked vaguely like a piece of popcorn on Tuesday morning.

### System Requirements

- One shiny wrapper from a piece of candy.

## 🔍 Troubleshooting: Deep Space Complications
---
When operating heavy avian machinery in zero gravity, look out for these common errors:

- **Error: Lost in the Atmosphere**
  You flew directly into a clean glass window reflection. Simply sit on the pavement looking slightly embarrassed for two minutes to reboot your tracking software.
- **Error: Fuel Tank Depleted**
  You haven't eaten a carbohydrate in over twenty minutes. Locate the nearest sidewalk pretzel immediately or risk automatic emergency shutdown.
- **Error: French Fry Jam**
  A rival pigeon has successfully intercepted the payload. Do not engage in diplomacy; puff up your chest feathers and run awkwardly toward them while making low guttural noises.
